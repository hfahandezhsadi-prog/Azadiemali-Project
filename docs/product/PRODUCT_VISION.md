## 1. Business Definition — Current State

Azadiemali is a business currently focused on educating the Iranian and Persian-speaking community living in Germany about real estate. Its main subject areas include buying, selling, renting, investing in, maintaining, optimizing and renovating property.

Azadiemali's role extends beyond publishing general educational content. The business seeks to identify interested audiences, clarify their path into the German real estate market through initial calls and messages, and provide educational and consultation services suited to their personal-use or investment goals.

Current services include online classes, consultation and mortgage financing referrals. These current services do not by themselves define the full scope of the future platform.

## 2. Current Target Audience

Two broad groups can be identified in the current business description:

1. Persian speakers who need to buy, sell, rent, maintain or renovate property in Germany for personal use.
2. Persian speakers who want to enter the German real estate market as investors or develop their investment activities more professionally.

More detailed audience segmentation, needs, knowledge levels, financial capacity and the journey of each group still require further analysis and approval.

## 3. Current Business Problem

Azadiemali currently operates as a content-driven and relationship-driven business. It uses general educational content to build trust and demand, then directs that demand toward online classes, consultation and mortgage referrals.

The main current problem is fragmented information and operations across Instagram and WhatsApp, together with the absence of a coherent lifecycle for leads, sales, scheduling, service delivery and follow-up.

## 4. Platform Purpose — Future Direction

The purpose of the Azadiemali platform is to create an integrated environment for managing the audience journey from first contact through service delivery and subsequent follow-up. This environment should connect customer information, education, consultation, financing referrals and related operations, and reduce reliance on manual recording and follow-up.

This purpose establishes the intended direction. It does not by itself determine the detailed target architecture or the complete scope of the future platform.
